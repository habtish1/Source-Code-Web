How we work

A delivery process that removes surprises

The same five steps on every project, whether it is a two-week bot or a two-year platform.

01

Discover

Users, workflows, constraints and what success looks like. You get a scoped plan with cost and timeline.

02

Design

Flows and screens first, then the system design: data model, integrations, infrastructure.

03

Build in sprints

Two-week sprints with a demo at the end of each. Working software early; priorities steered by evidence.

04

Harden & launch

Load tests, security review, store submissions, monitoring and runbooks before launch day.

05

Operate & grow

We keep running what we built: on-call, upgrades, new features and the data to decide what comes next.

Engagement models

Fixed-scope delivery

A defined product, a written scope, a fixed price and date. Best for well-understood builds such as websites, bots and MVPs.

Dedicated squad

Two to six engineers plus a lead, embedded with your team on a monthly retainer. Best for platforms that keep evolving.

Fractional CTO & consulting

Senior technical leadership, audits and roadmaps without a full-time hire. Best before a large budget is committed.
